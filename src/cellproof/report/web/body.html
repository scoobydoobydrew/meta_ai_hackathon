The cell

A damaged cell can pause, age, recycle itself or die.

This is the published network plus the four kinases the agent added. Switch DNA damage on, click a gene to knock it out or force it on, and see which fates remain. Every state shown was enumerated exactly by the solver. Nothing on this page is a simulation.

The agent

Read, propose, verify, repair.

Language models read well and guess with confidence. Solvers check exactly and cannot read. CellProof gives each the job it is good at and logs every step.

Where the claims go

Most of what a paper says does not become a rule. Each stage below removes what cannot or should not be used.

Limits

What this does not show.
